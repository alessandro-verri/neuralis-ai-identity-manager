## What it does

Your WooCommerce store already contains your product information. The add-on automatically creates and synchronizes the matching Neuralis entities, connecting your catalog to the Knowledge Graph managed by Neuralis Pro. Maintain your catalog in WooCommerce instead of entering the same information twice.

### Create product entities automatically

Published simple and variable WooCommerce products become Neuralis entities. Product information, prices and stock availability come from WooCommerce. Initial catalog synchronization runs in the background; another synchronization can be requested from the integration settings.

### Give every variation its own identity

Variable products are represented as a ProductGroup connected to individual Product variations. Each variation has a navigable Knowledge Graph page and applicable offers. Additional offers can point to internal or external pages and can be assigned to selected variations.

### Bring approved reviews into the graph

Approved native WooCommerce reviews become connected Neuralis Review entities. Changes are synchronized, and imported review entities are removed when their source reviews are deleted or are no longer approved. Existing manually created Neuralis reviews remain independent.

### Enrich product offers

Available identifiers, attributes, images and measurements enrich the product representation. Supported WooCommerce shipping configurations and the return policy you configure can be attached to store offers. Additional manual offers do not automatically inherit store policies.

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.0 or later.
- PHP 7.4 or later.
- WooCommerce installed and active.
- Neuralis AI Identity Manager Pro 1.2.0 or later, with Pro functionality enabled.
- A separate activated Neuralis for WooCommerce license.

The add-on extends Neuralis Pro. It is not a standalone replacement for WooCommerce or Neuralis.

## Installation and synchronization

1. Install and activate WooCommerce and Neuralis Pro.
2. Purchase the add-on and download its ZIP from Freemius.
3. Upload and activate the add-on in WordPress, then activate its license.
4. Allow the initial background catalog synchronization to complete and inspect the generated product entities and Knowledge Graph pages.
5. Continue managing source products and native reviews in WooCommerce. Request a catalog synchronization from the integration settings when needed.
